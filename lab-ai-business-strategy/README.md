## 🎯 Included Use Cases

### 1. [Use Case 1: Automated Insurance Claims Processing & Fraud Detection Engine](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Documents/GitHub/ai-solution-architecture/lab-ai-business-strategy/use-cases/use-case-1-claims-processing/business-strategy-roadmap.md)
* **Domain**: Insurance / Financial Services
* **Core Problem**: Slow claim settlement cycles ($14$ days TAT), manual document extraction bottlenecks, rising fraudulent payout leakage ($4.2M$ annually).
* **AI Solution**: Multimodal LLM document extraction, automated risk scoring, anomaly detection, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) claim triage.
* **Financial Return**: **Unadjusted ROI**: `240%` | **Risk-Adjusted ROI (r-ROI)**: **`162%`** | **EMV**: **`$1,215,000`** | **Payback**: `4.2 Months`.

### 2. [Use Case 2: Enterprise Omnichannel Customer Support Co-Pilot & STP Engine](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Documents/GitHub/ai-solution-architecture/lab-ai-business-strategy/use-cases/use-case-2-customer-support-copilot/business-strategy-roadmap.md)
* **Domain**: E-Commerce & Retail Operations
* **Core Problem**: High support ticket volume ($85,000$/month), high first-response latency ($18$ hours), agent burnout ($38\%$ annual turnover), rising cost per ticket ($12.50$).
* **AI Solution**: Hybrid SLM/LLM ticket intent routing, entity extraction, agent co-pilot assistant, and Straight-Through Processing (STP) for low-risk requests.
* **Financial Return**: **Unadjusted ROI**: `280%` | **Risk-Adjusted ROI (r-ROI)**: **`204%`** | **EMV**: **`$1,530,000`** | **Payback**: `3.6 Months`.
