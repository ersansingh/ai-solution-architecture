# Enterprise AI Problem Statement: Automated Insurance Claims Triage & Fraud Detection

## Executive Context
* **Industry / Sector**: Property & Casualty (P&C) Insurance / Financial Services
* **Business Unit**: Claims Operations & Underwriting Risk
* **Current Operational Scale**: 120,000 claims processed annually across auto and property coverage lines.

---

## 1. Core Business Problem Statement
Apex Insurance Corporation is experiencing significant operational degradation in its P&C claims handling workflow:
1. **Prolonged Claim Settlement Cycle Time**: The average turnaround time (TAT) to settle a standard claim is **14.5 days**, leading to poor customer satisfaction scores (CSAT drops to 62/100).
2. **High Processing Overhead**: Manual inspection and document verification require 4.5 hours of claims handler labor per file, resulting in an average processing cost of **$185 per claim** ($22.2M annual operational expenditure).
3. **Fraud Leakage**: An estimated **$4.2M in fraudulent payouts** escape detection annually due to sample-based manual audit procedures ($< 8\%$ of total claims manually audited for fraud).
4. **Handler Burnout & Error Rate**: Claims adjusters face a 12% manual error rate in document data extraction (handwritten forms, repair estimates, medical bills).

---

## 2. Desired Business Outcomes
* Reduce average claim processing TAT from **14.5 days to $< 48$ hours** for standard claims.
* Achieve a **50% Straight-Through Processing (STP) rate** for low-risk, high-confidence claims ($< \$2,500$ payout).
* Reduce claims processing cost per claim from **$185 to $< $75** (60% operational cost reduction).
* Detect and block an estimated **$2.5M+ in fraudulent claims** prior to disbursement via automated multimodal risk scoring.
