# Enterprise AI Problem Statement: Omnichannel Customer Support Co-Pilot & STP Engine

## Executive Context
* **Industry / Sector**: E-Commerce & Retail Operations
* **Business Unit**: Customer Experience (CX) & Operations
* **Current Operational Scale**: 85,000 incoming support tickets per month across web, mobile chat, and email channels.

---

## 1. Core Business Problem Statement
GlobalRetail Inc. faces severe customer support operational challenges due to rapid order volume growth:
1. **High Support Response Latency**: The average First Response Time (FRT) is **18.2 hours**, with average resolution time stretching to **42 hours** during peak shopping seasons.
2. **Escalating Support Cost per Ticket**: Handling tickets manually requires 220 full-time support representatives at an average cost of **$12.50 per ticket** ($12.75M annual customer support expenditure).
3. **Agent Turnover & Burnout**: Support agents suffer from repetitive, low-complexity inquiries (WISMO - "Where Is My Order?", returns, exchange policies), leading to a **38% annual agent turnover rate**.
4. **Inconsistent Quality & Routing Errors**: 22% of tickets are initially miscategorized or routed to the wrong specialist department, causing customer frustration and low CSAT (65/100).

---

## 2. Desired Business Outcomes
* Automate intent classification and entity extraction for **100% of inbound tickets** within $< 5$ seconds.
* Achieve a **45% Straight-Through Processing (STP) rate** for routine inquiries (WISMO, address updates, standard returns) without agent intervention.
* Provide a real-time **Agent Co-Pilot Assistant** for remaining complex tickets, cutting Average Handle Time (AHT) from 12 minutes to $< 4$ minutes.
* Reduce average cost per ticket from **$12.50 to $< $4.20** (66% operational cost savings).
* Lift Customer CSAT score from **65 to $\ge 85$ points**.
